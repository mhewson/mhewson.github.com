Pile of Shame Slayer sit down. roll. paint. repeat.

How it works: add your unpainted units (or import a spreadsheet), then hit Roll for a project, a Deck of Many Colors card and a technique. Log what you paint with a photo and the paints you used, filter by project, manufacturer or material, and start a deadline challenge like Miniac's The Finisher. Everything lives only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, so use Backup at the bottom to keep a copy.

📥 Import from a spreadsheet

Columns: Name, Total minis, and optionally Done, Manufacturer, Material (Plastic, Metal or Resin), Date bought (YYYY-MM-DD) and Projects (separate several with ;). In Google Sheets, select the cells and copy, then paste below — or upload a CSV (File → Download → .csv). A header row is fine.

Backup & restore

Copy your whole pile (including dates and badges) and keep it somewhere safe. To restore, paste it into the import box above and hit Import.

Share this app

Know another painter? Save this app as a single file you can email or message to them. They just double-click it to open it in their browser. It works without internet, and their pile stays on their own device.
